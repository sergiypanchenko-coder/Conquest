How it plays

Gold, squad, steel

If you have played a lane-defence game before, you already know how. Every soldier is a real type of fighter from history, and every battle is a real one.

1

Gather gold

Financiers and Medici bankers mint coins, and gold falls from the sky. Tap it to collect.

2

Choose your squad

Pick five to seven soldiers before each battle. New recruits join almost every time you win.

3

Hold every lane

Waves grow bigger and harder. Each lane has one cannon as a last resort. Lose a lane twice and the wall falls.

The campaign

One road through history

Battles sit on a real map, joined by one continuous route: from the mountains of Asturias, through the Holy Land, Georgia, the Danube and Ukraine, across the Mediterranean, to Pakistan, Syria and Iraq. Difficulty climbs with every battle.

The soldiers

Twenty-five fighters, one line

Each soldier copies a classic garden-defence role, armed with kit from real history. Like plants in PvZ, they are balanced by cost: cheap walls, steady shooters, and costly specials on long cooldowns.

Financier50

Makes gold

Renaissance moneylender. Wars ran on borrowed gold.

Shield wall

Roman legionary, 1st c. AD, with his curved scutum.

Greek hoplite, 5th c. BC: bronze helmet, aspis shield, dory spear.

Boss battles

Giants that carry their own army

Five bosses arrive last, after the final wave. At half health they throw the fighters riding on their backs onto the field.

Emir Ilghazi

Didgori, Georgia, 1121

The Artuqid emir who led the Seljuk coalition against David IV the Builder. He rides an armoured warhorse with two ghulam bodyguards on its back.

Sultan Osman II’s Great Bombard

Khotyn, Ukraine, 1621

The young sultan rode out in person. His giant bronze siege gun rolls forward firing, janissaries riding on the barrel.

Bin Laden’s Tank

Abbottabad, Pakistan, 2011

Destroy the tank and Osama bin Laden, leader of al-Qaeda, climbs out of the hatch and runs.

ISIS T-55 Tank

Baghuz, Syria, 2019

A captured tank defends the last stronghold of the self-declared caliphate.

Al-Baghdadi’s Car Bomb

Barisha, Syria, 2019

The final mission, at night. When the armoured truck falls, Abu Bakr al-Baghdadi, leader of ISIS, runs out in a suicide vest.

Final mission

Barisha, 26 October 2019

The campaign ends at night, under flares and searchlights, with its own opening titles and its own music. The leader of ISIS, Abu Bakr al-Baghdadi, is cornered. Hold every lane and let none escape.
